# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with locally vendored GSAP and ScrollTrigger. The project must remain easy for the student to edit directly in GitHub.

## Users

General-public visitors, including people with limited prior knowledge of biodiversity loss, encountering the work as a self-paced digital narrative.

## Product Purpose

THE LAST ONE makes extinction emotionally legible through the story of the last known individual or last confirmed record of six species. Success means a visitor can follow the chronology from 1844 to the present, understand each loss as an individual story, and leave with the question of which species could be next.

## Positioning

Instead of presenting extinction as a collection of statistics, the project uses scroll, time, habitat, individual portraiture, and archival evidence to make each disappearance feel singular and progressively closer to the present.

## Operating Context

The experience is a linear scrollytelling website. Visitors primarily scroll, use a persistent timeline for orientation and chapter jumps, and optionally expand each species story without leaving the chronology.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six ordered species chapters: Great Auk, Passenger Pigeon, Thylacine, Kauaʻi ʻōʻō, Baiji, and Pinta Island Tortoise / Lonesome George.
- A fixed vertical timeline remains on the left.
- Habitat imagery occupies the full chapter background; a separate species card represents the animal or last known individual.
- Details expand inline beneath the species card and may later contain text, archival images, audio, or video.
- The ending accelerates from 2012 toward 2026, flashes recent-extinction placeholders, and resolves to “NEXT?”.
- Placeholder media is acceptable until verified, credited assets are supplied.
- Motion uses GSAP and ScrollTrigger, with a functional reduced-motion experience.
- The site must remain a small, framework-free project that can be edited on GitHub and opened locally.

## Brand Commitments

The title is THE LAST ONE. The voice is quiet, restrained, factual, and elegiac rather than sensational. The supplied proposal and the UN Women historical timeline reference are binding structural references; the left-side timeline remains specific to this project.

## Product Principles

- Make one life carry the weight of a species-level loss.
- Preserve chronological orientation throughout the experience.
- Let atmosphere support factual storytelling without obscuring it.
- Keep content and media replaceable so research can be added incrementally.
- Make the complete narrative usable without relying on animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus, semantic headings, adequate contrast, and reduced-motion support are required. Placeholder and future media need meaningful alternatives or captions where appropriate.
